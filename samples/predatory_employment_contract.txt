EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT & RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

Section 1. Position and Devotion to Duties
Employee agrees to faithfully serve Company on a full-time basis. Employee shall devote one hundred percent of their professional time, energy, and skill exclusively to the business of the Company.

Section 2. Overbroad Non-Compete and Worldwide Industry Ban
Employee agrees that Employee shall not directly or indirectly engage in any competing business, provide consulting services to, or become employed by any entity operating in the same industry anywhere in the world for a period of three (3) years following termination of employment for any reason.

Section 3. Total Intellectual Property Inventions Assignment
Employee agrees that all inventions, concepts, designs, copyrights, algorithms, and writings created by Employee, whether created during or outside of working hours, and whether using Company equipment or personal devices, shall belong exclusively to Company as work made for hire. Employee irrevocably assigns all moral rights and worldwide titles to Company.

Section 4. Broad Indemnification by Employee
Employee shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Company from any and all damages, operational losses, customer disputes, or third-party liabilities resulting from Employee's performance of duties.

Section 5. Unilateral Changes to Compensation and Terms
Company reserves the right to modify job duties, compensation structures, commission plans, and discretionary bonus calculations at any time without notice in its sole discretion.
